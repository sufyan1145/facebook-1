Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Sufyan PostFire ("the Service", "we", "us") lets a user automatically publish video and image files from their own Google Drive to their own Facebook Page(s), YouTube channel(s), and Pinterest account(s) on a schedule they control, and offers optional AI-assisted editing and content-generation tools for that same content. This policy explains what data we access, why, and how it is stored and protected.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: the name, email address, and password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text) you provide, or use to log in with.
  • Google Drive data: with your explicit authorization, we access the folders and files you select in order to list them and upload them to Facebook, YouTube, or Pinterest. We request read-only access to your Drive — we cannot edit, delete, or share your files, and we do not access files outside the folders you connect.
  • Facebook Page data: with your explicit authorization, we access the list of Facebook Pages you manage, and we publish posts to the specific Page(s) and at the times you configure. We do not read your personal Facebook profile, timeline, messages, or friends list.
  • YouTube channel data: with your explicit authorization (via Google OAuth), we access the YouTube channel(s) connected to your Google account solely to upload videos on the schedule you configure. We do not access your other YouTube activity, subscriptions, or viewing history.
  • Pinterest account data: with your explicit authorization, we access the boards on your connected Pinterest account(s) so you can choose where Pins are created, and we create Pins on your behalf on the schedule you configure. We do not access other users' boards or your personal Pinterest feed/activity.
  • TikTok content: the Service can download a video from a TikTok link you provide, for you to review, edit, or repost through the Service. We do not connect to or authenticate with a TikTok account to do this.
  • Content processed by AI features: if you use optional AI-assisted features (e.g. auto-generated captions, titles, dubbing, or narration), the relevant text/audio/video is sent to our AI providers (Google's Gemini and Vertex AI services) solely to generate that content for you. This data is used to fulfill your request and is not used by us to train any model.
  • Usage data: we keep logs of actions taken in your account (e.g. "schedule created", "video uploaded", "login") so you can review your own activity history inside the app.

2. How we use your information

  • To operate the core function of the Service: moving content from your Drive folder to your connected Facebook Page, YouTube channel, or Pinterest account on your schedule.
  • To provide optional AI-assisted editing and content-generation features you choose to use.
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To send you account-related email (e.g. password reset) when needed.
  • To show you your own upload history, queue status, and activity logs inside the dashboard.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your Drive, Facebook, YouTube, Pinterest, or TikTok data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than providing the Service to you.

3. Data storage and retention

Account data and activity logs are stored in a private database. OAuth access tokens for Google, Facebook, and Pinterest are stored encrypted and are only used to make the API calls needed to run your schedules. Video and image files are downloaded temporarily to process an upload and are deleted immediately afterward — we do not keep a permanent copy of your files on our servers. Consistent with Pinterest's API terms, board and Pin data retrieved from Pinterest is not cached or stored beyond what's needed to complete your request in the moment.

4. Sharing of information

We do not share your personal data, Drive contents, Facebook data, YouTube data, or Pinterest data with third parties, except:

  • With Google, Meta (Facebook), and Pinterest, solely to perform the actions you've authorized (reading your Drive files, publishing to your Page/channel/board).
  • With our AI providers (Google Gemini/Vertex AI), solely to generate AI-assisted content you've requested.
  • Where required by law.

5. Your choices and control

  • You can disconnect your Google Drive, Facebook Page, YouTube channel, or Pinterest account connection at any time from Settings, which revokes our access.
  • You can delete individual schedules or your entire account by contacting us.
  • Revoking access via your Google Account, Facebook App settings, or Pinterest App permissions immediately stops the Service from accessing that account.

6. Security

Passwords are hashed, sessions use signed authentication tokens, and third-party access tokens are encrypted at rest. Access to the Service requires an account created for you — the Service is invite-only and not open to public self-signup.

7. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed at children and is not knowingly used by anyone under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

9. Contact

For any privacy questions, deletion requests, or to request an account, please contact us directly.
