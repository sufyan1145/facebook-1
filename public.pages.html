Log in to x.com in your browser → press F12 → Application (Chrome) → Cookies → https://x.com → copy the value of auth_token (ct0 is not needed). The cookies are checked with X first, then stored encrypted. If you log out of X in that browser, or X resets the session, the account shows "Expired" and you must add it again. This is an unofficial method: keep posting volume modest (a few posts a day) to protect your account.

Official Pinterest login (no cookies). After "+ Add Pinterest", choose the board the Pins go to. Website Automation can then turn every new article into a 2:3 Pin with an SEO title, description and the article link. While the Pinterest app has only "Trial" access, the Pins are visible only to you; they become public after Pinterest approves "Standard" access.

Official Threads login (no cookies). Website Automation can post every new article on Threads with an AI caption and the first picture; the article link goes in the post or, if you choose, in a reply to it. The login stays valid for 60 days and is renewed automatically while the app is used.
Add Pages by Page token
Connect Pages without a Facebook login: paste the Page access tokens. Easiest: in Graph API Explorer run me/accounts (permissions pages_show_list, pages_manage_posts, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_engagement), then paste the whole JSON answer here. You can also paste one line per page: PAGE_ID|PAGE_TOKEN (or just the token). Up to 1000 pages at once. Only add Pages you manage. Use a long-lived token, then it does not expire.
